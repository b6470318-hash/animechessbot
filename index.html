    How to play

    Click a move or use the arrow keys to review the game; tap the board to return. To premove, make your move while Lelouch is thinking; tap an empty square to cancel it. Right-click and drag to draw an arrow, or right-click a square to circle it. Hold Shift, Alt, or both for red, blue or yellow; left-click the board to clear them. On a touch screen, press and hold a square for half a second, then drag for an arrow or let go for a circle; tap the board to clear. Moves in gold are Lelouch's king walks.

    Lelouch, Ayanokoji, Aizen, Joseph, Jotaro, Gojo, Sukuna and Jesus run on Stockfish 10, weakened using the skill settings Stockfish's own formula assigns to each rating. Lelouch is also steered toward king moves when they cost little; Ayanokoji plays straight chess and prefers quiet moves when they're just as good. Joseph plays gambits like the King's Gambit and prefers checks and captures when they're nearly as good. Okuyasu mostly plays random moves and grabs any piece he can reach. Special abilities can be switched off on each bot's card. Owen, once a game, makes you play a move that hangs a knight, bishop or rook, and once a game pushes all his pawns forward at once, taking any piece straight ahead except a queen or king. Once a game: Lelouch's Geass turns one of your rooks, bishops or knights to his side; Ayanokoji quietly swaps two of your pieces; Okuyasu's The Hand erases a piece (sometimes his own). Malik, once a game, uses Aura to take one of your moved pieces (never a queen). Aizen, once a game, shows a fake move; after you reply, his real move is revealed and your reply stands only if it's still legal. Joseph, once a game, plays your next move for you (never a queen move). Gojo and Sukuna play Stockfish's weakest setting and, once a game, erase a random piece of yours that has moved (Hollow Purple and Malevolent Shrine). Goku plays like Okuyasu but steadier, and once a game fires a Kamehameha that removes one of your pawns. Jesus plays at full strength and, twice a game, uses his turn to lift one of your pieces off the board. Jotaro can stop time three or four times a game and move twice in a row, but can't capture anything during a stop; the skipped turn shows as a pause mark in the moves and as -- in the PGN. The ratings are close estimates, and a slow device can make the stronger bots play somewhat weaker. With a clock, they think faster as their time runs low. Pieces: cburnett set by Colin M.L.
